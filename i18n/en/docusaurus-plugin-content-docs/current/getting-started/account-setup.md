# Creating Your Clinic Account

This guide walks Dr. Amara Osei — a family medicine physician opening a solo practice, Riverside Family Health — through creating a DemoMed account. The same steps apply whether you're a solo provider or setting up DemoMed for a multi-physician clinic.

## 1. Sign up

1. Go to [app.demomed.example/signup](https://app.demomed.example/signup).
2. Enter your work email address. Personal email domains (Gmail, Yahoo, etc.) are allowed during trial but must be replaced with a verified organizational domain before you can invite staff.
3. Choose **I'm setting up a new clinic** rather than **I was invited to join a clinic**.
4. Set a password that meets DemoMed's minimum requirements (12+ characters, at least one number and one symbol).
5. Verify your email using the six-digit code sent to your inbox. The code expires after 15 minutes.

## 2. Choose a plan

| Plan | Providers included | Video minutes/mo | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Solo** | 1 | 1,000 | Independent practitioners |
| **Practice** | Up to 10 | 6,000 | Small to mid-size clinics |
| **Network** | Unlimited | Custom | Multi-location groups |

Dr. Osei selects **Solo**, since Riverside Family Health has one physician and one medical assistant. You can upgrade plans at any time from **Settings → Billing → Plan**; downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

## 3. Identity verification

Because DemoMed handles protected health information (PHI) and issues video visit links tied to licensed providers, every clinic owner must complete identity verification before the account is activated for live patient traffic:

- Upload a government-issued photo ID.
- Upload proof of medical licensure (NPI number is auto-validated against the NPPES registry).
- Confirm your practice's business address.

Verification is usually completed within a few hours. You'll receive an email once your account moves from **Pending Verification** to **Active**. Until then, you can still explore the dashboard and configure settings in sandbox mode — you just won't be able to send real video invitations to patients.

## 4. Next steps

Once your account is active, continue to [Setting up your clinic profile](/getting-started/clinic-profile) to configure your practice details, hours, and branding.
